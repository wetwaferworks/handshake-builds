HandshakeOS v4-full for the Anbernic RG35XX 2024 — flash instructions
=====================================================================
Image: v4-full, packed 2026-11-19 11:13 PM America/New_York.

This image is for a SPARE microSD card (TF1). Do not overwrite the
stock card. Back that card up first (see board/rg35xx-2024/windows-backup-stock-sd.ps1
in the HandshakeOS repo).

Play wins ties: the machine that already plays games stays on the
stock card until you are happy with this one.

Flash
-----
1. Open Rufus, Raspberry Pi Imager, or balenaEtcher.
2. Select handshakeos-rg35xx-2024-v4-full.img (the raw file, not the .zst).
3. Select the SPARE microSD card. Triple-check the drive letter.
4. Write the image. Eject the card safely.
5. Plug the card into the RG35XX 2024 (TF1 slot).
6. Power on. U-Boot loads /boot.scr from the card and boots Linux 7.3.5
   with the v4-full initramfs; the HandshakeOS GAMES picker comes up on
   the 640x480 panel.

After flashing, Windows will see one FAT32 drive labelled HANDSHAKEOS.
Put your own legally owned games in the ROMS folders (see ROMS/README.txt).
Do not rename the files in the root of that drive.

The handheld cannot be flashed over USB. USB FEL only boots it from RAM;
this image has to be written to the card in a card reader.
